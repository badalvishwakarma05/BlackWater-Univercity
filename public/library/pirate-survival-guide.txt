PIRATE SURVIVAL GUIDE (ABRIDGED)
A practical companion for the academically shipwrecked
=======================================================
(A fictional document from a fictional university. Advice may
nonetheless be accidentally useful.)

1. SURVIVING THE VIVA
   - Know your own project. Every plank of it.
   - If you do not know an answer, say so. Then say how you would
     find out. Examiners respect a navigator more than a liar.
   - Do not bring the parrot. The parrot has failed three vivas.

2. SURVIVING GROUP PROJECTS
   - Split the work on day one, in writing.
   - Commit early, commit often, and write commit messages that
     describe the change, not your emotional state.
   - "Moral support" is not a deliverable.

3. SURVIVING EXAM WEEK
   - Sleep. A rested pirate outscores a caffeinated one.
   - Past papers are treasure maps. Use them.
   - Thirteen barrels of coffee is a statistic, not a target.

4. SURVIVING THE CAFETERIA
   - The stew is the same stew. Choose accordingly.
   - Do not ask what is in the stew. The Kraken prefers privacy.

5. SURVIVING THE LIBRARY AFTER 10 PM
   - You won't.
   - The library is closed... forever.
   - (It reopens at 6 AM. Allegedly.)

Fair winds,
  -- The Blackwater Survival Committee (est. last Tuesday)
