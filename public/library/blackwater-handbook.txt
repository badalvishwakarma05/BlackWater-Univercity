THE BLACKWATER STUDENT HANDBOOK
(abridged, waterlogged, and entirely fictional)
=================================================

Welcome aboard, new crew member.

This handbook is a work of satire. Blackwater University does not exist,
which has never stopped it from issuing rules.

---------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 1. YOUR RIGHTS
---------------------------------------------------------------
1.1  You have the right to attend lectures.
1.2  You have the right to remain confused.
1.3  You have the right to one (1) rum ration per semester.
     The rum is gone. This right is therefore theoretical.

---------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2. YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES
---------------------------------------------------------------
2.1  Attend at least the required percentage of classes.
     The captain will not tell you the percentage. Use the
     attendance calculator on the Lower Decks.
2.2  Do not feed the database after midnight.
2.3  Do not let the parrot touch production.
2.4  Never deploy on a Friday.

---------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 3. DECKS YOU MUST NOT ENTER
---------------------------------------------------------------
- The Mysterious Restricted Basement
- The faculty cabins outside office hours (office hours are a myth)
- The second treasure vault (which does not exist)
- The crow's nest during attendance inspections

---------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 4. GETTING HELP
---------------------------------------------------------------
Ring the Mutiny Hotline. A parrot will answer. Whether the
parrot writes anything down is between the parrot and the sea.

The code is more what you'd call guidelines than actual rules.

  -- The Office of the Captain (acting) (still)
